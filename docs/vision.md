# Pigeon Vision

## One-Sentence Idea

We are building Pigeon to turn chess scoresheets into a **lifelong digital archive**—every game from every board, organized by event, with stats and tools to learn from your own history.

## Problem

Over-the-board chess still relies heavily on handwritten notation. After a tournament, many players either never enter their games or spend a long time typing them manually. This is worse for juniors, clubs, schools, and local tournaments where games are valuable but rarely archived.

**The gap is not just digitization—it is preservation.**

- Top boards sometimes get recorded; **chessmat and lower-section players are left out**.
- Chess.com and Lichess are for analysis of a few games, not a full event archive.
- FIDE and public databases reflect rated sections, not every player's complete tournament history.
- Paper scoresheets get lost; even the player forgets their best games over time.

## Who We're Building For

- **Players** who want every game saved quickly, organized by event (NC Opens 2024, Mombasa Opens, club nights).
- **Coaches** who review student games across many tournaments with comments and starred games.
- **Tournament organizers** who bulk-scan after rounds and review on desktop with AI legality hints.
- **Clubs and schools** running regular events where every section deserves a record.
- **Arbiters and volunteers** digitizing stacks of scoresheets after an event.

## Our Core Promise

Take a photo or scan of a scoresheet, turn it into PGN faster than manual entry, let the user correct mistakes, validate against chess rules, and **save the game forever** in an event-organized library with dashboard stats and replay.

## What Makes Us Strong

- **Lifelong archive** — not one-off scans; your whole chess history in one place.
- **Every board matters** — built for players who never appear in FIDE databases.
- **Event-first organization** — tournaments as folders, not a flat list of files.
- **Personal dashboard** — openings, win rates, streaks, top rivals.
- **Coach and organizer workflows** — permission-based sharing and bulk review.
- **Human review is product** — correction is fast and expected, not hidden.
- **Real chess workflow** — serious players, coaches, and TDs, not casual browsing.

## Our Principles

- Accuracy and trust matter more than flash.
- Human correction is part of the product, not a failure.
- We keep user games **private by default**; sharing and coach access are explicit.
- We make exporting easy; Pigeon is the archive, not a walled garden.
- Zero-explanation UX: if it needs a manual, we simplify the design.
- Welcoming, analysis-friendly visual design users want to return to.
- We build for players at every skill level and every section.

## Long-Term Vision

We want Pigeon to become the default place where over-the-board games live: scan in bulk at tournaments, organize by event, coach from shared libraries, and export anywhere. Revenue follows retention and organizer adoption—not before the core archive story works.
